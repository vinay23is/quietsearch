About this instance

Quietsearch is a private, self-hosted search service for a single user. It runs the open-source metasearch engine SearXNG behind a hardened HTTPS reverse proxy.

How it works

SearXNG is a metasearch engine: it has no crawler and no index of its own. Each search is sent, from this server, to several search providers at once. Their results are merged, duplicates are combined, and pages that several providers rank highly are moved up.

Providers see a request from this server, not from your browser. They don't receive your IP address, cookies, or account, so they can't tie your searches to you. They do still see the search terms. This is not anonymity. The privacy page explains exactly what is and isn't protected.

Google is available but off by default, because it blocks requests from servers. Use it for a single search by starting the query with !go. You can turn providers on or off for your browser under Preferences.

Defaults on this instance

  • SafeSearch: moderate (changeable in Preferences)
  • Autocomplete: off, so partial queries aren't sent anywhere as you type
  • Image previews: loaded through this server, not directly by your browser
  • Appearance: light or dark, following your device setting
Search is powered by SearXNG, free software licensed under the AGPL-3.0. This instance runs an unmodified SearXNG release; its configuration, deployment and these pages are maintained separately.
